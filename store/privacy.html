CompactDocs privacy policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

CompactDocs adjusts the layout of Google Docs document pages. It runs locally in your browser and reads page interface elements as needed to find panel controls and adjust the view. It does not collect or transmit your document content.

CompactDocs saves your panel and page-fitting preferences in Chrome's local extension storage. These settings stay in your browser's extension storage and are used to apply your choices when you open a document.

CompactDocs does not use analytics, make network requests, sell data, or share data with third parties. Google Docs and Chrome operate under their own privacy policies.
